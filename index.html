House Rules
··· Dot-Dot-Dot
Roll all dots — every other player pays you 1 chip. Players with no chips are exempt.
L–C–R Jackpot
Roll one L, one C, one R (any order) — pay 2 chips each direction (left, center, right) in order until you run out.
L–L–L Triple Left
Roll three L’s — pay 6 chips left instead of 3.
R–R–R Triple Right
Roll three R’s — pay 6 chips right instead of 3.
C–C–C Triple Center
Roll three C’s — pay 6 chips to the pot instead of 3.
Lifetime Bank
Chip Memory
Players carry their chip total across games. Winners keep their winnings. Losers never go below 0.

How to Play

Each player starts with chips. On your turn, roll one die per chip you hold (max 3 dice).

L
Pass one chip to the player on your LEFT
C
Put one chip into the CENTER pot
R
Pass one chip to the player on your RIGHT
·
A dot — keep your chip, nothing happens

If you have no chips, skip your turn but stay in the game. The last player with chips wins the pot!
